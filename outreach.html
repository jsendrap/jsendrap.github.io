Mathematics with Mindful Attention

Winner of the Mathematical Sciences Sponsorship Fund under the framework “Mathematics for All: Cultivating Inclusive Excellence”. The project develops inclusive resources in higher education and research for students with ADHD, and hosts a doctoral thesis she co-supervises at Universidad Politécnica de Madrid.

Featured audiovisual publication in the Computational Art section of Maple Transactions, on how to move from images of the real world to images built from Bohemian matrices.
