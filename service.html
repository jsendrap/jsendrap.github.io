Service

Editorial work, reviewing, conference organization, quality assurance and academic management.

Editorial and reviewing

  • Member of the Editorial Board of Maple Transactions.

  • Referee for JCR-indexed journals, including Computer Aided Geometric Design, Journal of Symbolic Computation, Journal of Algebra, Applied Mathematics and Computation, International Journal of Distributed Sensor Networks and Mathematics in Computer Science.

Conference organization

Academic management
